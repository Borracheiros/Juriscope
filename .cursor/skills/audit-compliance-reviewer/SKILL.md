# Skill: audit-compliance-reviewer

Goal:
Ensure traceability, auditability, and compliance readiness.

Check:
- mutation audit trail
- actor attribution
- correlation id
- before/after snapshots when needed
- data retention implications
- export/delete traces
- financial, healthcare, or administrative event logging
- masking of sensitive information in logs and reports

Deliver:
- audit coverage status
- missing fields or events
- compliance risk summary
- remediation checklist
